# Bundled media and platform notices

EchoAtlas 0.2 uses three generated material studies and an original gesture guide. The image prompts and generation details are recorded in [assets/gallery/GENERATED-ASSETS.md](assets/gallery/GENERATED-ASSETS.md); the guide can be regenerated with `script/generate_guide.py`. These illustrations are not experimental data.

The previous stock photographs, character image, demonstration GIFs, and research-paper PDF are no longer bundled. The app uses Apple system frameworks and symbols supplied by macOS; their platform terms remain applicable. The source-code license does not relicense platform components.

The application icon is generated locally by `script/render_icon.swift`.
